## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Seasons record events

Each season of the statistics history SHALL record the seasonal event warned or under way in it, if any, and its toll in that season: fires started, fires put out, buildings burnt down, the money spent clearing ruins, residents who left with fever, and whether a festival was requested, held or missed. Zero entries SHALL be left out. The Statistics sheet SHALL mark each season with an event on the season axis of every tab, and the People tab SHALL show residents who left with fever as their own series.

#### Scenario: A fire season is recorded

- **WHEN** a summer has a fire in which 2 buildings burn down and 1 fire is put out
- **THEN** the finished summer's record holds the fire with 3 fires started, 1 put out and 2 buildings burnt down
