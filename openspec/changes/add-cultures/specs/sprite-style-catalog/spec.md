## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Culture building variants

The catalog SHALL hold procedural variants of the house (operational and both upgraded tiers), the town center, the warehouse and the library for the Mediterranean, East Asian and Middle Eastern cultures, drawn in the building register with each culture's style, and each variant SHALL pass the content gate.

#### Scenario: Every culture variant is catalogued

- **WHEN** the catalog is loaded
- **THEN** it has an entry for each of the six buildings in each of the three non-default cultures
