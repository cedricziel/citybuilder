## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Temple garden knowledge flows into research

Knowledge added by temple gardens SHALL accumulate and flow into the current research like resident and library knowledge.

#### Scenario: Temple speeds up research

- **WHEN** Milling (40) is the current research with no progress, and 100 ticks pass with a single citizen house of 6 residents within 6 tiles of a served temple garden and no library
- **THEN** Milling's progress is 18
