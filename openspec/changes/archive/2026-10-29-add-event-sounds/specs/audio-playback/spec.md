## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Game events have sounds

The shipped bindings SHALL bind each of these events to at least one one-shot cue on the `sfx` bus: `buildingDemolished`, `placementRejected`, `bankruptcyWarning`, `bankruptcyResolved`, `seasonChanged`, `historyEvent`, `ageAdvanced`, `rivalAgeAdvanced`, `scenarioWon`, `monumentCompleted`, `fuelRanOut`, `commissionStarted`, `marketTraded`, `tradeCompleted` and `caravanSold`. Every bound file SHALL be generated by the project's sound generator. The three trade events SHALL share a set of at least three coin sounds, one picked at random per trade. `rivalAgeAdvanced` SHALL play the age peal more quietly than `ageAdvanced`.

#### Scenario: Every listed event has a sound
- **WHEN** the shipped bindings are loaded
- **THEN** each listed event has a start cue on the `sfx` bus whose file the generator's manifest entries name

#### Scenario: Trades pick among coin sounds
- **WHEN** the shipped bindings are loaded
- **THEN** `marketTraded`, `tradeCompleted` and `caravanSold` each have the same three or more coin cues

#### Scenario: A rival's new age sounds farther away
- **WHEN** the shipped bindings are loaded
- **THEN** `rivalAgeAdvanced` plays the same file as `ageAdvanced` at a lower volume
