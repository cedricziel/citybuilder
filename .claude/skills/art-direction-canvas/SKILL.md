# Art direction on a canvas

Draft v0. Rules get added after each round.

## Scene recipe

Draw the same scene in every direction so they compare fairly: town center,
6–8 houses, sawmill, lumberjack hut, forester, fields, roads, a forest edge,
a coast with pier and boat, walkers and carts.

## Judge on

Warm light, terrain blending, building density and scale, visible life.
