## Function

The Renaissance look of the Mediterranean residential dwelling and its two
upgrades. Shown in place of `building-house-mediterranean`, `building-house-tier2-mediterranean` and `building-house-tier3-mediterranean` in the Renaissance age when the player's culture is Mediterranean. Construction stages stay
shared with `building-house`. No operational animation.

## Visual identity

Rendered by IsoKit in the shared light rig. An ochre stucco palazzo on a stone base with pale cornices, blue shutters and a low tile hip roof. Same 2×2 footprint and bottom anchor as `building-house`.

## Sheet

Grid: 4 cols × 2 rows. Default sheet size.

- (0, 0): `building-house-renaissance-mediterranean` — a two-storey palazzetto
- (0, 1): `building-house-tier2-renaissance-mediterranean` — a three-storey palazzo
- (0, 2): `building-house-tier3-renaissance-mediterranean` — a broader three-storey palazzo
- (0, 3): spare
- (1, 0): spare
- (1, 1): spare
- (1, 2): spare
- (1, 3): spare

## Animation

None: finished sprites only. Construction uses the shared `building-house-constructing-*` frames.
