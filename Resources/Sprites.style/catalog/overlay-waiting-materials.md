## Function

Status badge over a construction site that is waiting for materials: the goods it needs are short
on the island, but a producer supplies them. It disappears once construction resumes.

## Visual identity

A small clock face on a dark disc, drawn as 16 × 16 pixel art and kept crisp when scaled.
