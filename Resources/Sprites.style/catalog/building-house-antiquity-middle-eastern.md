## Function

The Antiquity look of the Middle Eastern residential dwelling and its two
upgrades. Shown in place of `building-house-middle-eastern`, `building-house-tier2-middle-eastern` and `building-house-tier3-middle-eastern` in the Antiquity age when the player's culture is Middle Eastern. Construction stages stay
shared with `building-house`. No operational animation.

## Visual identity

Rendered by IsoKit in the shared light rig. Three steps up: a mud-brick hut with a reed roof deck, a rendered house behind a parapet and a yard wall with a date palm, and a courtyard house with a columned porch; roof-beam ends show under the parapets. Same 2×2 footprint and bottom anchor as `building-house`.

## Sprites

- `building-house-antiquity-middle-eastern` — a mud-brick hut with a reed roof
- `building-house-tier2-antiquity-middle-eastern` — a rendered house behind a yard wall, with a palm
- `building-house-tier3-antiquity-middle-eastern` — a courtyard house with a columned porch and a palm

## Recipe

- Recipe: `building-house-antiquity-middle-eastern` (IsoKit)
- Also renders: `building-house-tier2-antiquity-middle-eastern`, `building-house-tier3-antiquity-middle-eastern`
- Cell: 2×2 footprint, 96 pt tall (tier 2 112 pt, tier 3 112 pt)
- Construction stages: 0
- Operational frames: 0
