## Function

The Renaissance look of the East Asian residential dwelling and its two
upgrades. Shown in place of `building-house-east-asian`, `building-house-tier2-east-asian` and `building-house-tier3-east-asian` in the Renaissance age when the player's culture is East Asian. Construction stages stay
shared with `building-house`. No operational animation.

## Visual identity

Rendered by IsoKit in the shared light rig. A town house with a dark lattice shopfront, a pent roof and a white plastered upper storey under slate. Same 2×2 footprint and bottom anchor as `building-house`.

## Sprites

- `building-house-renaissance-east-asian` — a two-storey town house
- `building-house-tier2-renaissance-east-asian` — a broader town house with an upper balcony
- `building-house-tier3-renaissance-east-asian` — a merchant's residence under a large slate hip roof

## Recipe

- Recipe: `building-house-renaissance-east-asian` (IsoKit)
- Also renders: `building-house-tier2-renaissance-east-asian`, `building-house-tier3-renaissance-east-asian`
- Cell: 2×2 footprint, 144 pt tall (tier 2 144 pt, tier 3 160 pt)
- Construction stages: 0
- Operational frames: 0
