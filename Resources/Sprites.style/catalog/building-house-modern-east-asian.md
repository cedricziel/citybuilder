## Function

The Modern look of the East Asian residential dwelling and its two
upgrades. Shown in place of `building-house-east-asian`, `building-house-tier2-east-asian` and `building-house-tier3-east-asian` in the Modern age when the player's culture is East Asian. Construction stages stay
shared with `building-house`. No operational animation.

## Visual identity

Rendered by IsoKit in the shared light rig. Rendered concrete with blue glass bands and a dark panelled ground floor under a slate hip roof canopy. Same 2×2 footprint and bottom anchor as `building-house`.

## Sprites

- `building-house-modern-east-asian` — a one-storey house
- `building-house-tier2-modern-east-asian` — a two-storey house
- `building-house-tier3-modern-east-asian` — a five-storey block under the canopy

## Recipe

- Recipe: `building-house-modern-east-asian` (IsoKit)
- Also renders: `building-house-tier2-modern-east-asian`, `building-house-tier3-modern-east-asian`
- Cell: 2×2 footprint, 112 pt tall (tier 2 144 pt, tier 3 240 pt)
- Construction stages: 0
- Operational frames: 0
