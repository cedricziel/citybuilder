## Function

The Modern look of the Middle Eastern residential dwelling and its two
upgrades. Shown in place of `building-house-middle-eastern`, `building-house-tier2-middle-eastern` and `building-house-tier3-middle-eastern` in the Modern age when the player's culture is Middle Eastern. Construction stages stay
shared with `building-house`. No operational animation.

## Visual identity

Rendered by IsoKit in the shared light rig. Rendered concrete over a sandstone ground floor with a blue band, a lattice sun screen and a small blue dome. Same 2×2 footprint and bottom anchor as `building-house`.

## Sprites

- `building-house-modern-middle-eastern` — a one-storey house
- `building-house-tier2-modern-middle-eastern` — a two-storey house
- `building-house-tier3-modern-middle-eastern` — a five-storey block

## Recipe

- Recipe: `building-house-modern-middle-eastern` (IsoKit)
- Also renders: `building-house-tier2-modern-middle-eastern`, `building-house-tier3-modern-middle-eastern`
- Cell: 2×2 footprint, 112 pt tall (tier 2 144 pt, tier 3 224 pt)
- Construction stages: 0
- Operational frames: 0
