## Function

The autumn look of the forest tile. The renderer swaps it in for
`terrain-forest` while the calendar is in autumn.

## Visual identity

Autumn canopy: wheat-lit crowns over sun-terracotta and thatch leaves with terracotta shade, on a floor of thatch and light-loam leaf litter. Same tree layout as `terrain-forest`.

## Sheet

Grid: 1 cols × 1 rows. Static — no animation. Cell is 512×512 sheet
pixels.

- (0, 0): `terrain-forest-autumn` — canonical static tile

## Animation

Static — no animation.
