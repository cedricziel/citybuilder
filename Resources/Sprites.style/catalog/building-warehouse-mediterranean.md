## Function

The Mediterranean look of the bulk-goods storage building. Shown in place of
`building-warehouse` when the player's culture is Mediterranean.

## Visual identity

Rendered by IsoKit in the shared light rig. A long whitewashed storehouse under a low hipped tile roof, two arched cargo doors, amphorae and a crate.
