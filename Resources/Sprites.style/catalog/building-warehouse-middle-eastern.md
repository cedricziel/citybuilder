## Function

The Middle Eastern look of the bulk-goods storage building. Shown in place of
`building-warehouse` when the player's culture is Middle Eastern.

## Visual identity

Rendered by IsoKit in the shared light rig. A sandstone store with a parapet roof, a tall arched gate framed in blue tile, a striped awning, cargo and a date palm.

## Sprites

- `building-warehouse-middle-eastern` — canonical operational base
