## Function

A market hub, smaller and cheaper than the town center, so a town can grow beyond one hub. It holds house goods, and its porters carry them to the houses in its reach and come back with empty baskets (visible-distribution, milestone 2b).

## Visual identity

Rendered by IsoKit in the shared light rig. An open market hall on timber posts over two awning stalls with produce, a half-timbered upper floor with two windows under a steep tile gable, on a cobbled plot with baskets and a crate. Open on every side, so porters come and go from any road.
