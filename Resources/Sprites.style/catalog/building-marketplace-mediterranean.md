## Function

The Mediterranean look of the marketplace, the market hub smaller than the town center. Shown in place of
`building-marketplace` when the player's culture is Mediterranean.

## Visual identity

Rendered by IsoKit in the shared light rig. An open hall on whitewashed piers over two stalls with ochre and cream awnings, under a low tile hip roof on a plastered beam, on a travertine plot. Same 2×2 footprint and bottom anchor as `building-marketplace`.

## Sprites

- `building-marketplace-mediterranean` — canonical operational base
