## Function

Cloth producer. Carriers bring wool; the weaver turns 2 wool into 1 cloth.

## Visual identity

Rendered by IsoKit in the shared light rig. A half-timbered hall under slate with a red lean-to on its sunlit east side sheltering a floor loom, red cloth on its beam. Lengths of madder, weld and woad cloth dry on a line across the front yard, and a great spinning wheel stands by the door.
