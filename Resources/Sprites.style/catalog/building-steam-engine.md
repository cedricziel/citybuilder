## Function

Industrial age signature. Burns charcoal; while fuelled it doubles the speed of workshops within 6 tiles and smokes houses within 4 tiles. It only animates while fuelled.

## Visual identity

Rendered by IsoKit in the shared light rig. A brick engine house under slate, a tall round brick chimney, a rocking beam on a stone bob wall over a pump head, and a coal heap.

## Sprites

- `building-steam-engine` — canonical idle base
- `building-steam-engine-constructing-0` — stone pad
- `building-steam-engine-constructing-1` — timber frame and a half-built chimney
- `building-steam-engine-constructing-2` — brick walls with scaffolding
- `building-steam-engine-operational-0` — beam up with a smoke puff
- `building-steam-engine-operational-1` — beam down
