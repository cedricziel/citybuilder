## Function

UI icon for the `wool` good. Used in the goods bar and building
tooltips. Single static cell.

## Visual identity

A cream fleece tied round the middle with twine. Hand-authored SVG on a 24-point grid, in the style every goods icon shares: natural colours in three tones, lit from the upper left like the world, inside a dark warm outline (`#2B1E15`) that keeps the silhouette readable on light and dark HUDs.
