## Function

Sends its watchman along the road to put out a fire within 8 tiles; he
douses it for 30 ticks. Answers one fire at a time.

## Visual identity

Rendered by IsoKit in the shared light rig. A timber lattice tower on a
single tile: a rubble footing, four corner posts braced in two storeys, a
lookout under a tiled hip roof with a bronze bell, a lantern that lights at
night, a ladder on the south face, and a water barrel and a leather bucket
at its foot. Design canvas: https://claude.ai/artifact/NfTHPHsBMu4gKzjgvxYfz5
(Fire watch, option A).
