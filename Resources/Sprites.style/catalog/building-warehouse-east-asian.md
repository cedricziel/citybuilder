## Function

The East Asian look of the bulk-goods storage building. Shown in place of
`building-warehouse` when the player's culture is East Asian.

## Visual identity

Rendered by IsoKit in the shared light rig. A kura storehouse: white plaster walls over a dark slate plinth, a heavy timber door, a long slate gable roof, and rice bales in the yard.

## Sprites

- `building-warehouse-east-asian` — canonical operational base
