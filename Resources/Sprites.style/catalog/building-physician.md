## Function

Keeps houses within 8 tiles safe from fever while it is fuelled: it
burns 1 herbs every 100 ticks, but only while a fever is warned or under
way, so it keeps its stock in quiet years.

## Visual identity

Rendered by IsoKit in the shared light rig. A half-timbered house under a
tile roof with blue shutters, herb bundles drying under the eave, a
hanging sign with a bronze mortar, a lavender bed and a drying rack.
Windows light at night. Design canvas:
https://claude.ai/artifact/NfTHPHsBMu4gKzjgvxYfz5 (Physician, option A).
