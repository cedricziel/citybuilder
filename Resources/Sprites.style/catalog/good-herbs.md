## Function

UI icon for the `herbs` good: the physician's fuel against fever. Used in
the goods bar, the town market and building tooltips. Single static cell.

## Visual identity

A bunch of leafy green sprigs tied with twine, two lavender tips among
them. Hand-authored SVG on a 24-point grid, in the style every goods icon shares: natural colours in three tones, lit from the upper left like the world, inside a dark warm outline (`#2B1E15`) that keeps the silhouette readable on light and dark HUDs.
