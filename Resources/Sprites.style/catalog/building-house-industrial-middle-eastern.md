## Function

The Industrial look of the Middle Eastern residential dwelling and its two
upgrades. Shown in place of `building-house-middle-eastern`, `building-house-tier2-middle-eastern` and `building-house-tier3-middle-eastern` in the Industrial age when the player's culture is Middle Eastern. Construction stages stay
shared with `building-house`. No operational animation.

## Visual identity

Rendered by IsoKit in the shared light rig. Brick blocks behind parapets with pale caps, a striped awning and brick chimneys. Same 2×2 footprint and bottom anchor as `building-house`.

## Sheet

Grid: 4 cols × 2 rows. Default sheet size.

- (0, 0): `building-house-industrial-middle-eastern` — two storeys
- (0, 1): `building-house-tier2-industrial-middle-eastern` — three storeys
- (0, 2): `building-house-tier3-industrial-middle-eastern` — four storeys with a lattice bay
- (0, 3): spare
- (1, 0): spare
- (1, 1): spare
- (1, 2): spare
- (1, 3): spare

## Animation

None: finished sprites only. Construction uses the shared `building-house-constructing-*` frames.

## Recipe

- Recipe: `building-house-industrial-middle-eastern` (IsoKit)
- Also renders: `building-house-tier2-industrial-middle-eastern`, `building-house-tier3-industrial-middle-eastern`
- Cell: 2×2 footprint, 128 pt tall (tier 2 160 pt, tier 3 192 pt)
- Construction stages: 0
- Operational frames: 0
