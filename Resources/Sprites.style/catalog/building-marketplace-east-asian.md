## Function

The East Asian look of the marketplace, the market hub smaller than the town center. Shown in place of
`building-marketplace` when the player's culture is East Asian.

## Visual identity

Rendered by IsoKit in the shared light rig. An open hall on red-lacquered posts over two stalls with indigo and cream awnings, under a wide-eaved slate hip roof, on a gravel plot. Same 2×2 footprint and bottom anchor as `building-marketplace`.

## Sprites

- `building-marketplace-east-asian` — canonical operational base
