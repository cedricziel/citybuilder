## Function

The Middle Eastern look of the marketplace, the market hub smaller than the town center. Shown in place of
`building-marketplace` when the player's culture is Middle Eastern.

## Visual identity

Rendered by IsoKit in the shared light rig. An open hall on sandstone piers over two stalls with blue and ochre awnings, under a parapet roof with two blue domes, on a sand plot. Same 2×2 footprint and bottom anchor as `building-marketplace`.

## Sprites

- `building-marketplace-middle-eastern` — canonical operational base
