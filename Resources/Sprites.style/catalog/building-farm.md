## Function

Food producer. Grows grain on its 2×2 footprint with no inputs; a
carrier hauls the harvest to the nearest road-connected goods buffer,
where houses draw it to satisfy their food need.

## Visual identity

Rendered by IsoKit in the shared light rig. A plank barn under a thatch roof in the back corner, cabbage beds in dark soil filling the front, a crate and a barrel by the door.

## Sprites

- `building-farm` — canonical operational base (ripe field, roofed barn)
- `building-farm-constructing-0` — tilled soil, stone pad for the barn
- `building-farm-constructing-1` — seedlings, barn timber frame
- `building-farm-constructing-2` — green crops, barn walls without roof
- `building-farm-operational-0` — ripe wheat, wind band at rest
- `building-farm-operational-1` — ripe wheat, wind band rolled forward
