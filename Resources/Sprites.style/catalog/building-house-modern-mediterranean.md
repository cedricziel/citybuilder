## Function

The Modern look of the Mediterranean residential dwelling and its two
upgrades. Shown in place of `building-house-mediterranean`, `building-house-tier2-mediterranean` and `building-house-tier3-mediterranean` in the Modern age when the player's culture is Mediterranean. Construction stages stay
shared with `building-house`. No operational animation.

## Visual identity

Rendered by IsoKit in the shared light rig. White rendered houses with blue glass bands and flat roofs with a roof-top pergola. Same 2×2 footprint and bottom anchor as `building-house`.

## Sheet

Grid: 4 cols × 2 rows. Default sheet size.

- (0, 0): `building-house-modern-mediterranean` — a one-storey villa with a pergola
- (0, 1): `building-house-tier2-modern-mediterranean` — a two-storey villa with a pergola and a pool
- (0, 2): `building-house-tier3-modern-mediterranean` — a five-storey block with balconies
- (0, 3): spare
- (1, 0): spare
- (1, 1): spare
- (1, 2): spare
- (1, 3): spare

## Animation

None: finished sprites only. Construction uses the shared `building-house-constructing-*` frames.

## Recipe

- Recipe: `building-house-modern-mediterranean` (IsoKit)
- Also renders: `building-house-tier2-modern-mediterranean`, `building-house-tier3-modern-mediterranean`
