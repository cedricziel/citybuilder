# Newcomer: I found the farm fast, then the game went quiet on me (2026-10-25)

This is the sample run that proved the `playtest` skill and the newcomer persona. It is short on purpose (about 14 real minutes, most of them waiting for a starved host), so treat it as a first-hour glance, not a verdict.

## Top findings (from the owner's questions during the session)

### 1. The start view is too close: I see a house and no island
- **What I see.** On the iPhone 17 Pro the new game opens with the town center filling about two thirds of the screen width (about 275 of 402 pt) and trees to every edge. No coast, no open building ground beyond a green patch, no second landmark. `playtests/2026-10-25-newcomer/start-sequence/zoom-1.0-default.png`, `first-screen-t2s-coachmark.png`.
- **Can I see where to build?** Barely. There is a stretch of grass under the town center, and nothing says that this is where houses and farms go. I built my farm on the first open green I saw. I could not tell how big the island is or that there is a coast.
- **Is pinch-out discoverable?** No. The only gesture the game teaches is "Long-press a tile to build" (the coachmark). No text, hint, First steps line or settings entry mentions pinch or zoom. I only knew to pinch because it is a normal phone gesture. It works: one pinch with two fingers from 200 pt apart to 100 pt apart took me to about half zoom and showed the coast, rocks and open ground. `zoom-about-0.5-after-pinch.png`.
- **What the code says.** Zoom range is 0.25 to 4.0 (`Camera.minZoom`/`maxZoom`, `Packages/CityCore/Sources/CityCore/Camera.swift:14-15`). A new game starts at zoom 1.0 on every device (`Camera(... zoom: 1.0)`, `World.swift:155`; `centerCameraOnHomeTownCenter` in `World+StartSites.swift:333` only sets the center). The title screen backdrop uses 1.2 on iPhone, iPad and Mac and 2.0 on Apple TV (`TitleBackdrop.swift:111-113`). At zoom 1.0 one tile is 64 pt wide on screen whatever the device, so an iPhone (402 pt) shows about 6 tiles across and an iPad Pro 11 (834 pt) about 13; the island is 96 by 96 tiles. The phone gets the same 1.0 as the iPad, so it gets half the view.
- **What feels right on a phone.** About 0.5 to 0.6 at the start: the town center is a third of the screen width, the coast and rocks show, and houses still read. At 0.25 the buildings turn to specks. A first view that shows the town center plus open ground around it would make the first step obvious.

### 2. Start sequence, frame by frame (iPhone 17 Pro, portrait, fresh install)
Frames were taken with `xcrun simctl io screenshot` in a loop (host heavily loaded, so spacing was 0.5 to 1 s). `start-sequence/title-launch-frames.png`, `new-game-to-first-screen-frames.png`.
- **Launch to title.** Home screen, then the title at about 12 s after launch on the loaded host: "Citybuilder" in large serif at the top, the town backdrop at zoom 1.2 drifting slowly (sin sway), buttons "New Game..." and "Settings" low on the screen. The backdrop town runs off the top left and right edges and the title word sits over its roofs. No intro card, no fly-in. It stayed this way for the 60 s I watched. `title-screen.png`.
- **New Game dialog.** A full-height sheet slides in. Seven sections fit; Start is at the bottom right. It lasts until I act.
- **Start to game.** A hard cut in under 1 s: the dialog dims in one frame (Start greyed), the next frame is the map. No zoom animation, no camera move, no fade. The HUD (status pill, pause, speed, "..." button, "First steps 0/5 · net $0" pill) is there from the first frame at its normal size: the "..." button is about 44 pt, the pill about 32 pt tall.
- **The only card is the coachmark.** "Long-press a tile to build" with a "Got it" button sits over the build bar from the first frame and stayed on screen for the 34 s I watched, until I tapped "Got it". Size at the default text size: about 290 by 45 pt, text about 16 pt, nothing clipped except that it hides the five build-bar labels. `first-screen-t2s-coachmark.png`, `first-screen-t34s-coachmark-still-there.png`.
- **Zoom during the sequence:** title 1.2, game 1.0, constant after the cut.

### 3. Bug: the coachmark, the "..." button and the First steps pill become huge at larger text sizes
- **What the owner saw.** "The intro card and the More button at the top right were super big." I could not reproduce it at the default text size (large): both look normal in every frame above. I could reproduce it with Dynamic Type set larger, so the likely cause is the simulator's or phone's text size.
- **Repro.**
  1. `xcrun simctl ui <UDID> content_size accessibility-extra-extra-extra-large` (also bad at `accessibility-medium`; smaller at `extra-extra-extra-large`).
  2. Fresh install (so the coachmark shows), New Game, Start.
  3. Look at the top right and the bottom of the screen.
- **What happens.** At accessibility XXXL: the "..." button grows to about 80 pt and covers the corner; the coachmark becomes a card about 300 pt wide and 200 pt tall, wrapping "Long- / press a / tile to / build" and swallowing the build bar; the First steps pill wraps to "First / step / s 0/5", splits "net $0" letter by letter and runs off the screen edge. At accessibility medium the pill wraps to three lines and the coachmark to two. The money and population pill (fixed font sizes) does not grow at all, so the HUD ends up out of proportion. `start-sequence/text-size-accessibility-extra-extra-extra-large.png`, `text-size-accessibility-medium.png`, `text-size-extra-extra-extra-large.png`.
- **Not a scale animation.** In 60 consecutive frames after Start nothing grew or shrank. No `matchedGeometryEffect` or scale transition sits on the More button; I looked for one. The size follows the text size.
- **Likely cause (file:line).**
  - The More button's symbol uses `.imageScale(.large)` with no explicit font, so it scales with Dynamic Type and its `.padding(8)` circle grows with it: `Packages/CityUI/Sources/CityUI/CityRootView+Pause.swift:70-73` (the same pattern is on the Research, Statistics, Minimap and Settings buttons in that file).
  - The First steps capsule uses `.font(.caption...)` text with no `lineLimit`, `minimumScaleFactor` or `fixedSize`, so it wraps and breaks mid-word at large sizes: `Packages/CityUI/Sources/CityUI/FirstStepsCardView.swift:28-38`.
  - The coachmark uses `.font(.callout)` and a `.borderedProminent` button inside a `Capsule` with no cap: `Packages/CityUI/Sources/CityUI/TouchPlacementViews.swift:100-114`.
  - Nothing caps Dynamic Type for the HUD (`grep dynamicTypeSize` in CityUI finds no use), while the status pill uses fixed `.system(size:)` fonts (`StatusPillView.swift`), so the two halves of the HUD scale differently.
- **Fix idea (not applied, per the brief).** Cap the HUD with `.dynamicTypeSize(...DynamicTypeSize.xxxLarge)` (or `.accessibility1`) and give the capsule and the coachmark `lineLimit` plus `minimumScaleFactor`. Please ask the owner whether their simulator or iPhone uses a larger text size, because that is the most likely cause of what they saw.

## Setup
- Device: iPhone 17 Pro simulator (402 × 874 pt), portrait. The persona says iPhone 17; the 17 kept crashing the control tool under load, so I used the Pro, which has the same screen size.
- Build: `41d46f5f` (branch `feat/playtest-harness`, on `origin/main` 574e76c4), debug build.
- Launch flags: none. Regular New Game from the title screen: Sandbox, Single Island, seed 0 (default), Northern European, Medieval, Normal.
- Played 15:47 to 16:01 real time. The game ran at 1× the whole time (it never pauses while you read), so about 14 game minutes passed: roughly 14 seasons. The HUD shows no date, so I only know that from the trees changing colour.
- Plus start-sequence and zoom checks after the main session. Ended by choice after the "problem" text on my farm; I had understood the first step but not why nothing else happened. I did not reach 3 houses.

## What was fun
- The island looks friendly. The town center with its tiny market stalls, the sheep that wander out as soon as the farm exists, and the autumn and winter colours made me smile. Seeing the season change by itself felt alive.
- Gather and Craft drawers show every building with a picture and a price. I could see what I could afford without reading a manual.
- The First steps card is a good idea. Five plain steps with a reason under each one. "Empty houses pay nothing" told me something I would never have guessed.

## Confusing or frustrating
- **"Tap or drag to place farm" is not what happens.** Screen: tool strip after picking Farm. I tapped the ground and got arrows and a check mark instead. Nothing said to press the green check. I only found it by trying.
- **The little numbers on the strip mean nothing to me.** Screen: tool strip, "6/2" next to a log icon. I did not know it was "wood I have / wood it needs". It changed to "4/2" after the farm and I still could not tell why.
- **The red icon over the farm tells me something is wrong but not what.** Screen: map, above the farm. Two different icons in two minutes, no words. The sentence I needed, "Its road doesn't reach a warehouse or the town cen-ter", only appears if I tap the farm, and nothing told me to.
- **The text I needed breaks mid-word.** Same callout: "town cen-ter". Also "Needs Cultivatio / n" on the locked Hop Garden tile in the Gather drawer.
- **The pop-up hides the thing I want to tap next.** Tapping the road opened "Road · State: operational · Road: connected · Details · Actions · Demolish" right on top of the farm and the red icon. I had to tap empty ground to get rid of it before I could tap the farm. `06-menu-and-road-callout.png`
- **"Road: connected" lies to a newcomer.** The road callout says "connected" and the farm callout says "Road: connected" too, and then the next line says its road doesn't reach the town center. To me "connected" meant done.
- **I do not know the date or the year.** There is no date on the phone HUD, yet the game reports a season and the whole screen changes colour. I could not tell how long I had been playing or how long a year is.
- **The game keeps running while I read.** I spent a minute on the First steps card and the seasons moved on. A pause button exists, but nothing suggested I should use it.
- **The "First steps" pill is small.** Grey text on grey, easy to read as decoration. I only opened it because the persona reads everything.
- **The coachmark sits on top of the build bar.** "Long-press a tile to build" covers the labels Town, Gather, Craft, Road and Demolish until I press "Got it". It also teaches long-press while the actual flow (tap a rail button, tap a drawer tile, tap the ground) never uses it.

## Bugs
1. **Stray Demolish pill.**
   - Start: new game, first screen, the Road tool armed.
   - Steps: tap at (300, 40), the empty status-bar area right of the dynamic island.
   - Expected: nothing, or a normal tap.
   - Happened: a glass pill labelled "Demolish" in red appeared over the status pill. It stayed until I tapped elsewhere. Seen once; I cannot say whether the tap or an earlier long press on the road tile made it. Screenshot `05-stray-demolish-popup.png`.
2. **Drag-to-place road does not show roads clearly.** Steps: arm Road, drag a short diagonal near the farm. Happened: money fell by $15 (three tiles) but the road looks like a faint smear that is hard to tell from the ground in winter and autumn. Not a rules bug; a readability bug.
3. **Mid-word line breaks** in the farm callout ("cen-ter") and in locked drawer tiles ("Cultivatio / n", "Needs Metallurgy" cut). Steps: tap the farm after laying a road that does not reach the town center; open Gather, look at the Hop Garden tile.

## Balance
- Start: $1.000, Pop. 0. Farm costs $60 (Normal), Road $5 a tile, Lumberjack $80, Forester $60, House $50, Warehouse $200, Library $100, Sawmill $120, Bakery $90.
- Wood stock reads 6 at the start; a farm needs 2 and a house needs planks I did not have a way to make.
- Net stayed $0 for the whole run: no houses, so no tax; the farm had no upkeep I could see. I never saw money go down except by building.
- I could not judge pacing. I only ever played step 1.

## Top 3 changes
1. Say what a red icon means in words, where I am looking, the moment it appears: a short bubble such as "Farm: no road to the town center" that I do not have to hunt for.
2. Make placing a building do what the strip says. Either tap places it, or the strip should say "Tap to position, then tap the check".
3. Put a date on the phone screen, or pause when I open a card, so I know how fast time is going.

## Fun rating
5/10. I would keep playing for a few more minutes because the farm step worked and the little town is pretty, but I would drop it the first time a red icon sat on my farm for two minutes with no explanation. I did not feel punished; I felt unsure.
